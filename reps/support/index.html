FAQ

  • Does REPS work offline?
    Yes, everything works in airplane mode after install.
  • Where is my data?
    Only on your phone. Reset in YOU → RESET APP DATA (this also empties the widget).
  • Language?
    YOU → LANGUAGE → DEUTSCH, ENGLISH or TÜRKÇE. You can also pick it in your phone's settings — iPhone: Settings → REPS → Language; Android 13+: Settings → Apps → REPS → Language. Both stay in sync.
  • Voice cues?
    YOU → VOICE CUES on or off.
  • Do timers keep running in the background?
    Yes — rest, EMOM and interval timers use the real clock and catch up after a screen lock.
  • Reminders?
    YOU → REMINDERS: pick days and time. Skipped if you already trained that day.
  • Reminders don't arrive?
    Allow notifications for REPS in your phone's settings (REPS shows a note if they're blocked). On Android also check that the category "Workout reminders" is on under Settings → Apps → REPS → Notifications.
  • Widget?
    Long-press the home screen → Edit → Add Widget (iPhone) or Widgets (Android) → add REPS. It shows your weekly goal and streak.
  • Widget empty or not updating?
    Open REPS once — the widget updates when you leave the app, and redraws by itself at the start of each week.
