Privacy Policy

v2.0.0, 2026

REPS ("the app") is published by Surenler LLC. Contact: murat@surenlerllc.com.

Controller: Surenler LLC, 8 The Green, Ste A, Dover, DE 19901, USA.

  1. WE COLLECT NOTHING. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no location access, and no cross-app tracking. We do not receive, store, or process your personal data on any server.
  2. ON-DEVICE DATA ONLY. Your workout history, settings, goal and level are stored locally on your device (browser localStorage inside the app) and never transmitted anywhere. Sharing only happens when you tap SHARE RESULT, which opens your OS share sheet — content goes where you choose; the result image is drawn on the device and only placed in the app's temporary cache for the share sheet. The home-screen widget reads a small summary the app saves in storage shared only with its own widget (iOS App Group / Android app storage): weekly goal progress, streak, the name of your last workout and the labels in your language. If you pick REPS's language in your phone's settings, your operating system stores that choice. None of this leaves the device.
  3. DEVICE FEATURES. The app can use Wake Lock (keep screen on during workouts), vibration, audio beeps and optional on-device speech synthesis for cues. All run locally; no audio or sensor data leaves the device. Optional workout reminders (off by default) are local notifications scheduled on your device by the operating system; no push service or server is involved, and you can turn them off in YOU → REMINDERS or in your phone's notification settings.
  4. CHILDREN. The app is suitable for general audiences and collects no data from anyone, including children.
  5. DELETION. Delete everything anytime: open the app → YOU → RESET APP DATA (or uninstall — uninstalling removes all on-device data). Resetting also empties the widget's summary.
  6. CHANGES. If this policy changes (e.g. we add optional cloud sync), the in-app policy and this page will be updated before any data is collected, and review notes will disclose it.
